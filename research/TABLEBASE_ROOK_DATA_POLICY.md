# Computed rook tablebase admission — tablebase-rook-data-v1

2026-10-11 namespace revision under TABLEBASE_DATA_POLICY.md. T002 is separate
from frozen T001 and public-game datasets. It admits only the20 exact WDL exports
in datasets/tablebase-rook-approved-sources.json: KRPvKR complete capture/promotion
closure, mechanical KK base case. No DTZ, arbitrary material, position API or game
export is admitted by this revision. The generator's explicit free redistribution
grant covers generated tables, not unrelated game data or probe software.

The new tablebase-rook-data-policy.mjs checks the public D001 test gate, complete
registry, exact HTTPS exports/sizes/hashes, immutable publisher permission and
checksum metadata BEFORE acquisition. Before probing it additionally verifies
the entire local acquisition manifest, all20 files, WDL magic and size/alignment,
original final URLs/origins and exact probe/vendor/runtime bindings. Missing or
ambiguous eligibility, changed bytes, unsupported material or an incomplete
dependency closure refuses. Guarded loaders have no bypass flag.

Keep complete source metadata, real acquisition times, byte hashes and raw probe
outputs. WDL50 is a zero-counter model, not an arbitrary actual-clock/history
outcome; neither WDL nor file checksums prove teaching usefulness. No DTZ/DTM,
best-move distance or real-game precision claim follows from this admission.
Source permission is recorded as Syzygy-generator-redistribution-permission,
not CC0 or a legal determination. Probe software retains separate GPL3+ license.

T001 registry/loader/manifest/files and public-data-v1 inputs/receipts remain
unchanged. T002 has its own hashes/version/receipt and cannot relabel an earlier
mock or KQvK panel as observed rook evidence. Future exports/material formats
require a prospectively documented admission revision and focused checks.
