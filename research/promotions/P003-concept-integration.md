# P003: Expanded local Concepts integration and concise player reply

- Date/decision: 2026-10-12, implemented locally under the user's explicit request
  to integrate available accepted and usable provisional research before the
  exhaustive historical audit finishes. No push or remote publication. This is
  implementation authorization, not scientific acceptance or measured benefit.
- Baseline: local main `22966e0`, P001/P002, E020–E080's optional worker, cache,
  player handling and main Concepts panel. Latest accepted tracker E082 remains
  378 occurrences / 328 names. Original P001 provenance and all previously
  promoted source bytes are preserved.
- Selected implementation: 98 additional detector entrypoints, one zero-credit
  E140 prerequisite stage and eight compatible later inspectors, with a
  300-module browser closure. Exact emitted provisional observations are exposed;
  broader candidate scope names do not become verified detectors. E081/E082 add
  only their six accepted mechanical labels. E176/E178/E180 scope projection is
  not counted as an independent detector. E221–E229 reference/support sessions
  are not detector coverage. No unsupported catalog implementation is invented.
- Inventory/evidence: [build-first workflow](../concepts/BUILD-FIRST.md),
  [current E082 tracker](../experiments/E082-rook-ending-facts/evidence/concept-status.md),
  [E260 occurrence census](../experiments/E260-original-occurrence-census/RESULT.md),
  [E270 retained combined checks and current semantic audit](../experiments/E270-combined-validation/RESULT.md),
  and [generated exact integration inventory](../../lib/concepts/integration-provenance.json).
  Research build status at inspection: 539 provisional scopes, 127 support,
  41 strategic/practical prerequisites; 162 ready batches, no stale batches.
  Historical combined 12,017-test evidence is reused, not rerun as a promotion
  prerequisite. E061 semantic work and E066/E067 boundary/terminal qualifications,
  E073's recorded definition discrepancy, original-scope semantic acceptance and
  real-game precision/usefulness remain open. No frozen evidence is rewritten.
- Interface: a small accessible two-button switch at the top of the existing main
  Concepts panel. All concepts retains every matched supported finding/details;
  Coach reply selects at most two substantive non-overlapping themes from the
  same results. Navigation, variation, pending/absent/unavailable/exhausted states,
  engine layout and Settings inspector remain connected to the existing session.
- Selection: urgency and consequences precede category. Actor/target/defender
  colors and exact support deltas drive ownership. Certified mates, defensive
  needs and conditional reply risks outrank raw geometric/structural features.
  Same-target new attack and undefended status may combine; pawn support loss
  requires the recorded ledger. Aliases add evidence, not repeated commentary.
  Inventories and unchanged structure abstain. Turn-flipped patterns remain
  hypothetical; no advantage, best move or winning capture is inferred from a
  contact or category. Empty replies are explicit. Heuristic selection quality is
  not empirically validated.
- Identity: unique username/saved name match controls `S.meSide`; orientation has
  its own state. Missing/ambiguous identity uses neutral all-view labels and
  withholds player-addressed advice. Sharing an anonymous game does not fabricate
  an identity from orientation/default color.
- Architecture: deterministic generated source/provenance registry, strict browser
  assertion adapter, exact-endpoint staged parent reuse, isolated optional worker,
  4,000-node stage caps, compact summaries and proof-panel reuse. Fast progress
  publishes at 125ms intervals; long stages reset the existing five-second
  inactivity watchdog. Failure retains completed evidence without caching
  unfinished analysis. Exact history and source/profile fingerprint invalidate
  cache entries; switching view/player does not repeat searches.
- Unavailable: declared alternatives/objectives/context or order followup,
  missing/full-origin history, complete certificate panels, real admitted
  tablebases/private exact-source tokens, sustained-policy/strongest-defense
  graphs and validated strategic/practical rubrics. Per-study blockers are in
  the machine inventory; [implementation guide](../../docs/CONCEPT-INTEGRATION.md)
  explains their interfaces. Ordinary Stockfish scores are not research panel
  certificates. No tablebase or support-file coverage is implied.
- Data exposure: existing retained research provenance plus authored synthetic
  integration/tactical fixtures and existing browser fixture games. No new game
  acquisition, server analysis, fit, scoring change or teaching-effectiveness
  claim. Operation remains local and opt-in.
- Regression/reproduction: source/promoted-byte hashes and browser import closure;
  real-opening staged vs original E177 event/evidence/status agreement; both colors
  and opponent moves; unknown identity/orientation/share; combination/priorities,
  alias suppression, proof horizon, abstention; navigation, perspective, variation,
  stale callbacks, timeout and shared cache. Production/source/build/browser/lint
  outcomes and observed costs are recorded below after completion.
- Rollback: restore P002 runtime/profile/presentation; remove new generated closure
  and P003 browser adapter/stage helpers. Existing research evidence, scoring and
  saved analyses remain intact. No release/push is authorized by this decision.
