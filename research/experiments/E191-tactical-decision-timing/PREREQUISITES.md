# Scope and retained prerequisites

These three candidates retain the original C0810/C0811/C0813 teaching meanings.
They supply narrow, causal decision evidence, not general timing competence.

- Attack: a legal checking capture answers a current checker and starts a full
  checking mating policy; the selected quiet block loses to certified enemy mate.
  Every legal defense in the selected policy is replayed. Discarded source search
  is also checked, rather than accepting a selected line alone. Nonchecking attacks,
  sacrifices, positional buildup and long-term attack timing remain open.
- Defense: the same actual move is a legal response to a real check, compared
  with a different legal response with opposite bounded outcomes. This is stronger
  than mandatory evasion but does not address nonchecking threats, prophylaxis,
  or defending positions without a finite mate contrast. The attack and defense
  positive examples overlap; distinguishing defense wins with nonchecking winning
  continuations is still a broader validation need.
- Structure: every actual reply moves a pawn, removes prior adjacent-file support
  of the original enemy pawn and permits its capture with a complete finite mate.
  A same-unit quiet alternative has a live, target-preserving, nonisolating reply
  that defeats all such target-capture mate goals at the same horizon. The quiet
  alternative might still win another way. The candidate makes no global value,
  favorable exchange, general pawn-break or human decision-quality claim.
