# Remaining scope and validation

E108 already provides outcome-based recapture comparisons for C1068, including
positive mate versus draw/enemy mate. E204 complements that with an explicit
finite conversion objective; it does not replace that evidence or infer an
alternative's global loss/draw from failed finite mate search.

C1068 general exchange quality still needs appropriate strategic outcomes,
alternatives, horizons and usefulness evidence. C1073 certifies only a live
checking forced-mate policy at the actual after-capture frame, with every saved
defending branch and checking attacking choice replayed. Sustained positional
initiative, quiet pressure and initiative outside this context remain open.

C1078 removes a specific profitable immediate capture policy relative to the
declared alternative before certified conversion. Profit uses the common original
root balance and every nonempty live counterreply. No assertion eliminates all
counterplay, positional resources or later defensive activity. Terminal immediate
mate does not establish live initiative or liquidation into a live rook ending.

General long-term/temporary advantages and advantage transformation require
timescale and outcome evidence; phase change alone is insufficient. The complete
teaching catalog and broader meanings remain in scope.

Shared chess.js and frozen E144/E177 source admission are limits on independence.
The own checker reconstructs phase, capture profit, checking policy and answers
without importing the own runtime, E190 classifier or E177 derive implementation;
the separate E190 checker validates the whole retained parent result.

Combined original-occurrence, absence, priority, terminal, claim, history and
budget integration; full inherited independent replay; changed main/repeat/clean
reproductions; eligible real-game precision, human usefulness and production
promotion remain deferred. Focused synthetic checks do not satisfy these gates.
