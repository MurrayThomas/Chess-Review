E189 required explicit utility/loss and a decision context before risk labels.
This callable supplies a declared nonprobabilistic contract for exactly two
complete finite policies from the same root/actor/history. Caller declares
ordered win/draw/loss utility, target outcome, maximum allowed shortfall and units.
For each certified outcome, shortfall=max(0,target utility minus outcome utility).
Worst-case shortfall is the maximum, not a frequency-weighted expectation.
Every ordinary defense and adverse outcome/shortfall stays visible. Unresolved
or illegal followup leaves withhold the complete comparison; they never become
draws or receive a risk score. Provided malformed source results are rejected.

C0797 assesses this explicit model and its two policy envelopes. C0831 additionally
requires the ACTUAL policy to meet the caller's loss limit while the reference
exceeds it, with strictly smaller actual worst loss. Both-inside/both-outside,
equal risk, reversed order or missing declaration do not establish management.
This is formal conditional choice relative to a declared reference. The model
is not calibrated to human utility or authenticated private preferences; no
probability, practical chances, global optimality or general strategic judgment.

Reuses exact E235 synthetic board Kf7 Qe5 versus Kh7, with both colors and genuine
recorded prefixes. Actual Qg7# gives utility2 with no shortfall against a win goal;
reference Qf5+ followed by fixed Qg6 covers EVERY defense with mate or stalemate,
giving worst shortfall1 under declared utility2/1/0. Allowed0 establishes the
bounded management contrast. Reversal withholds management; allowed1 or draw
target does too. Scaling utility/limit changes units consistently rather than
manufacturing probabilities. These are evidence of the mathematical contract,
not proof that a person should choose these utilities or tolerate those losses.

Source E235 permits extra draw claims while preserving nonloss. Risk arithmetic
cannot ignore them. Every live after-actual enemy turn conservatively adds a draw
choice if earlier history is incomplete or a known claim is available. Immediate
mate ends first. Extra draws are labeled as conservative allowances, separate
from positively certified ordinary leaves. Own optional claims may be declined.
Supplied histories are assumed ongoing; earlier adjudication/unrecorded automatic
repetition cannot be excluded from partial histories. No actual-game adjudication
claim is emitted. General/adaptive strategies and losing-policy role coverage
remain for combined expansion/validation, rather than forcing unresolved
nonterminal alternatives into the finite contract.
