# Scope of the second-weakness candidate

2026-10-11. Original C1079 asks whether a second weakness can be created. This
candidate proves two concrete pawn vulnerabilities at the same postreply frame:
an original isolated enemy pawn has a legal positive-gain capture through every
immediate live enemy response both at root and after each actual reply; a distinct
original supported pawn is newly isolated under every compelled support-pawn
move and permits a complete target-capture mate. The same-unit quiet alternative
has a live nonisolating escape from that specific target-exploitation goal.

The first-pawn ledger retains all captures, including losing Nxd2 and profitable
Qxd2 at root. The existence of one profitable capture suffices; every enemy
response to that capture must retain positive nominal gain. The vulnerability
is measured over this explicit immediate response contract, not full minimax
value. The second-target goal uses full finite mating policies and ALL defenses,
including queen/rook/bishop/knight promotions. H0 geometric vulnerabilities alone
do not establish the candidate. No unproved finite search failure becomes a loss.

First pawn survives at its original square through the structural reply. The
contract does not require both weaknesses to remain exploitable after choosing
and completing one target-capture plan. Lasting simultaneous exploitation,
defensive burden across two fronts, the principle of two weaknesses, nonmating
value, optimality, general strategy and human usefulness remain open. No scope
is deleted from the teaching catalog or promoted to final acceptance.
