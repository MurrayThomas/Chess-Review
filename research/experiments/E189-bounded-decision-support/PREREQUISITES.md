# Decision concepts whose broader requirements remain unmet

2026-10-11. No readiness credit for C0797/C0798/C0800/C0801/C0802.

| Original scope | Required evidence | Why current proof panels do not suffice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C0797 risk assessment | Explicit adverse outcomes/loss scale, decision context/utility, calibrated probabilities or a declared nonprobabilistic risk contract with independent validation | Mate certificates show certain finite outcomes, not risk tolerance or probabilities for unresolved choices. |
| C0798 practical chances | Eligible opponent/context/clock data, calibrated response model and usefulness evaluation | A losing proof can coexist with practical chances against fallible play; branch counts cannot estimate those chances. |
| C0800 subjective difficulty | Declared player/context population and independent difficulty or effort evidence | Legal breadth and proof size do not establish human difficulty. |
| C0801 general complexity | Prospectively validated complexity definition and relevant structural/decision features | One bound's node or branch count is an instrument feature, not the whole idea. |
| C0802 uncertainty | Explicit estimand, information/model assumptions, source of uncertainty and validated reporting | 'Unresolved within H' is bounded lack of proof; not a draw, probability interval, unclear position or generic uncertainty estimate. |

C0788 is a coach analysis TOOL supporting candidate selection, not an assertion
about candidates a player actually considered. C0799 covers certified mating
outcomes from an explicit perspective only, not a general position value. C0803
is a concrete same-bound mate/loss alternative contrast, not every critical
strategic decision. Those broad meanings remain pending in the complete catalog.

Mechanical terminal draws stay a separate shortlist partition and do not pass
this mate-only C0799 scope. Nonmating outcomes, whole-position quality, optimality,
statistical performance, teaching usefulness and human validity need suitable
additional proof/data/rubrics. Combined occurrence/absence/priority/history/budget/
terminal audits, source-bound saved replay and changed main/repeat/initially clean
reproductions remain required before acceptance; no long full suite here.
