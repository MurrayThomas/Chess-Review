# Piece-question scope and remaining work

C1063 Which pieces are badly placed? and C1065 What is my opponent's best piece?
gain declared-objective scopes only. C1064 What is my worst piece? already has
E151's complete immediate-freedom ranking; E203 adds a different functional
criterion without replacing that scope or declaring either a strategic rubric.
All original broad meanings remain required work.

The domain is EVERY current alive B/N/R/Q of the source actor, not its two nominated
equal-value units. Pieces with zero target attempts remain present. Complete
enemy responses and tracked-target captures/counters are frozen E156 evidence.
Coverage requires a live profitable tracked-pawn capture after EVERY enemy reply,
nonempty live immediate counters and >=1 net gain from baseline throughout.
Source-after is a genuine recorded frame: current history exactly appends the
source's quiet actual move. E200 supplies all original identities, including
earlier captured units and the relocated unit. Target/attempt/counter inventories
and every failure remain in the full bound source proof.

This binary criterion answers only a specified tactical objective. At least one
covering and one failing piece are needed for relative labels; all ties retained.
The both-nominee-cover source has a third unselected Ne1/reflected Ne8 that fails,
so the complete domain legitimately separates TWO covering ties from that unit.
An independent defender instead defeats the entire domain and withholds labels;
all-fail is not evidence that every piece is strategically badly placed.

C1063 names failing units for the declared color/objective only, not every badly
placed piece of both armies. C1064 needs the player's own color profile; C1065
needs the opponent's profile. Other-side requests explicitly abstain. Current
actor-to-move is retained and never flipped. All-unit goal coverage does not imply
best/worst under other goals, long-term value, general strategic usefulness,
optimal exchange choices or whole-position quality.

The source's two equal-value nominees remain an admission prerequisite even
though all units participate in the new profile. Arbitrary positions without a
compatible complete panel are unavailable; this is not a universal evaluator.
Dedicated all-cover domains, nonquiet/checking/terminal-source positive guards,
target advance/promotion/EP and promoted-piece identity matrices remain deferred.
Underlying source/E200 checks cover their respective mechanics but this focused
batch does not claim exhaustive new integration coverage. The genuine valid
clock-claim control is freshly retained: source root98, recorded actual99,
future quiet replies100; all labels withheld.

runtime imports, using E200's independent identity checker and frozen E156
semantic verifier with separate exact cache. Shared chess.js/source semantics
remain explicit. Declared work charges identity and exact source ledgers plus
every inspected unit/branch/attempt/matching-counter/routing operation. Atomic
exhaustion drops all evidence and labels; no wall-clock/internal-library bound.

Combined cumulative regression, complete independent inherited replay, original
occurrence/support/absence/priority/history/terminal/claim/budget matrices and
