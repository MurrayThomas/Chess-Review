C0467 now has a closed all-legal-defense nonchecking mate-threat policy scope.
A finite supplied policy is fully admitted, not inferred from observed repetition.
Every actor state renews a legal conditional mate-in-one threat in an explicitly
artificial turn-flipped diagram; every legal defender reply has its exact successor
inside the graph. No graph state is checked, mated, stalemated or recognized as
insufficient material. Unreachable padding, omitted defenses, illegal/counterfeit
threats and broken closure reject the certificate. Missing policy/history means
unavailable, not that perpetual attack is impossible.

times. Turn, placement, castling and legal EP are part of the identity; genuine
prefix occurrences can only shorten the bound. Earlier opponent claim or automatic
draw is compatible. This is an available draw resource until a claim/earlier draw,
not an infinite-play theorem, forced win, best move, necessity or player intent.
Current claimable positions withhold a new attack label. Declining optional claims
in actual history is allowed; continuing mate/automatic terminals is rejected.

earlier clock accuracy is an input assumption, not inferred from a truncated prefix.

clock counters for legal-edge admission and are explicitly history-free; the
deterministic sample replays actual full counters/history. That sample is one path
only. Universal scope follows from exhaustive graph closure and finite-state
recurrence, not from pretending the sample enumerates every continuation. The

it must never become a fictional recorded prefix.
