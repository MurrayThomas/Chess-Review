`applyStudyEvent` and `readStudySession`. Strict authored tasks support exact
board-state questions after a legal prefix, choose-move and line targets, and
ungraded reflections. Learning goals are objectives, not observed mental acts.

learning, intuition, overconfidence or psychological-pressure findings.

Private capability state retains keys, computed answers, feedback and optional
typed review material. Public views show prompts/root/prefix/questions/goals and
the learner's own committed answer. A separate reveal requires an existing
immutable submission. Earlier views expose no keys, grades or review context;
preparation totals remain hidden until all tasks reveal to avoid key-length hints.

tokens cannot authorize reads/events. Cloned inputs/events/views isolate state.

who supplied the input. Full synthetic research keys are deliberately retained.

not best play or a forcing/strategic proof. A legal different answer is disagreement,
not a new move-error classifier. Syntactically valid illegal attempts commit and
reveal explicit illegal-answer feedback later. Their full requested move lengths
still charge the budget. Reflections remain ungraded and self-reports, including
false values, remain labelled reported-not-observed.
